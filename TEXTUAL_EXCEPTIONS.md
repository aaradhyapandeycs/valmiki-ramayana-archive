# Sanskrit source exceptions

The source audit compares the pinned Sanskrit independently against raw HTML, then compares the generated and published text. A separate overlay restores 162 source paragraphs across 23 chapters. These comprise 144 ordinary verse paragraphs in Ayodhyā 85 and Yuddha 93–96, 16 colophons, the Araṇya 1 opening invocation, and the separately labeled Bāla 1 Gāyatrī appendix. The overlay preserves source order, wording, printed locators, and the chapter's pre-existing local anchors. Each restored paragraph has a hash and its own stable position-based anchor.
